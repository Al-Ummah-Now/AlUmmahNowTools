Rejecting, deleting, or signing out while imports run asks first and says what it does to how many videos. A deleted filter preset can be put back with Undo.
Videos filmed in HDR (for example on newer phones) are converted to normal colors when encoded on this computer; before, they could look too bright or pale.
Unfinished downloads (.temp.webm, .part and the like) are left out of a scan and listed; each can be added anyway. A file the video tools cannot read as a video is no longer uploaded.
